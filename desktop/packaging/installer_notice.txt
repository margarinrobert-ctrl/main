Trading Backtester

This application analyses historical market data on your own computer.

  * It performs no network requests of any kind.
  * It collects no telemetry and sends no data anywhere.
  * Your data files, strategies and backtest results are stored only in your
    workspace folder (by default Documents\TradingBacktester) and are left in
    place if you uninstall.

Backtested results are a description of what a set of rules would have done on
past data. They are not a prediction and not a guarantee of future performance.
Historical simulation systematically flatters strategies: it cannot reproduce
the queue position, partial fills, latency, liquidity or emotional pressure of
live trading. Treat every number this application shows you as an upper bound.
