
==========================================================================================================================
A. THE CONTRACT, AND WHAT A ROUND TURN COSTS
==========================================================================================================================
  Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (MNQ): $2.00 a point, tick 0.25 points = $0.50, so one tick is $0.50 a contract
  assumed price impact  1 tick spread + 1 tick slippage a side = $2.00 a round turn
  fees                  $1.44 a round turn (CME + NFA + clearing + broker)
  TOTAL ROUND TURN      $3.44 per MNQ contract = 1.720 points
  (the full-size NQ is $20 a point, so the same rule on NQ is 10x every dollar
   figure below with a round turn near $24 -- proportionally cheaper)

==========================================================================================================================
D. THE ARITHMETIC THAT DECIDES IT, IN MNQ TERMS
==========================================================================================================================

  as posted
    median risk           26.6 points = $53.12 a contract
    round turn            1.720 points = $3.44  (6.5% of risk)
    reward:risk           1.00   driftless break-even win rate 50.0%   ACTUAL 48.4%
    gross points / trade  -1.053 = $-2.11   net $-6.55
    break-even needs the win rate to rise +1.6 points

  retr 0.50 / stop 0.75
    median risk           26.8 points = $53.62 a contract
    round turn            1.720 points = $3.44  (6.4% of risk)
    reward:risk           2.00   driftless break-even win rate 33.3%   ACTUAL 36.0%
    gross points / trade  +1.119 = $+2.24   net $-2.20
    break-even needs the win rate to rise -2.6 points

  retr 0.50 / stop 0.75 locked             99      388     -1618             40.5              4854

  'DD in contracts' is the drawdown divided by 2% of a $2,000 micro account, i.e. how
  many 2%-risk units the drawdown consumes. MNQ day-trade margin is typically $50-100
  and intraday buying power is not the binding constraint here -- the drawdown is.
