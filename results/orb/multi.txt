  US30_ISO           15m           1              1.878          29.7%        522
  NQ                  5m           3              2.454          11.1%        765

  WHEN THE TRADING BAR IS THE OPENING RANGE, range/ATR IS ~1 BY CONSTRUCTION -- the
  range is one bar and ATR(14) is the average size of one bar. The [0.3, 1.5] band is
  therefore a band around 1, and it passes most sessions. On 5-minute bars the same
  band keeps the quietest ninth. The gate was written for a 15-minute chart.
