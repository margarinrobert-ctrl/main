3. WHAT AN 84% WIN RATE WOULD NEED
======================================================================================================================
  bar-close model, as configured: win 49.9%  avg win +9.3 pts  avg loss -23.4 pts  -> PF 0.395
  same avg win / avg loss at a 60% win rate -> PF 0.595
  same avg win / avg loss at a 70% win rate -> PF 0.925
  same avg win / avg loss at a 84% win rate -> PF 2.082
  PF 3.08 at 84% needs avg win / avg loss = 0.59 -- i.e. the +7 trail wins against -16 stops, but
  winning 5 times in 6. At the bar close this rule wins about 1 in 2. The extra wins have to come
  from ENTERING at prices the bar close does not offer: an intrabar touch of the EMA8 bought at
  the touch, then the trail locking +7 on the bounce. That is what an unguarded `strategy.entry`
  does with an intrabar execution option ticked, and it is why the script is guarded in v2.
